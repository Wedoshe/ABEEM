Tier 1 · E · Stress
Answer each question based on how often the participant experienced the situation.
1. Sleep disturbance
In the past month, how often have you had difficulty falling asleep or experienced broken sleep because of worries or tension?
2. Physical tension
In the past month, how often have you felt unexplained body aches, heavy headaches, or constant exhaustion because of stress?
3. Financial / livelihood anxiety
In the past month, how often have you felt constantly overwhelmed or deeply anxious about household expenses, farming, job security, or family responsibility?
4. Inability to relax
In the past month, how often have you found it difficult to sit peacefully, clear your mind, or feel calm even when your daily work is finished?
Proposed stress scoring:

0–4 → Low stress → +0
5–9 → Moderate stress → +10
10–16 → High stress → +20
Tier 1 · F · Adult participant section
This section is intended for adult screening participants. If the participant never drinks alcohol, select that option and the section will contribute 0 points.
1. Frequency
How often does the participant drink alcohol?
2. Quantity on a drinking day
Select the closest number of standard drinks usually consumed on a drinking day.
The supplied protocol does not specify a separate scoring row for exactly 5 standard drinks. This prototype therefore does not silently invent one. The scoring specification should be confirmed with the project supervisor before field deployment.
3. Five or more drinks on one occasion
How often does the participant have five or more standard drinks on a single occasion, such as at a festival, marriage, party or weekend?
Proposed alcohol scoring:

Total 0–3 → +0
Total 4–7 → +10
Total 8–12 → +20
